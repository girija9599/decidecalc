Contact

Found a bug, want a new calculator, or have feedback? We'd love to hear from you.

Report a bug or request a tool

Please include: the calculator name or URL, a short description of the issue, the inputs you used, the result you saw, and the result you expected.

Email: contact@decidecalc.com
We aim to reply within 3–5 business days.

Before you write

  • AdAccount/help questions — we can't reset your account because we don't have one. All tools work without login.
  • Lost data — because inputs are never sent to our servers, we cannot recover a past calculation.
  • Tax/financial advice — we cannot provide personal advice. See our Terms and consult a qualified professional.

Privacy note

Please do not include sensitive personal data (PAN, Aadhaar, account numbers, passwords) in your message. Our team replies by email and your message is handled per our Privacy Policy.
