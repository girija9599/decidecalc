Privacy Policy

DecideCalc is designed privacy-first. This page explains exactly what we do — and don't — collect.

No accounts, no login

DecideCalc has no user accounts. You never enter an email, phone number, or password to use any tool. There is no sign-up form.

Calculations stay in your browser

Every calculator runs entirely as client-side JavaScript. Your inputs (loan amounts, salary, dates, body measurements, etc.) and your results are never transmitted to our servers. We do not log, store, or read them.

Local storage

We store only your theme preference (light/dark mode) and other purely-local UI settings in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

Service worker / PWA

If you install DecideCalc as a Progressive Web App, the browser caches the pages locally so the app works offline. This cache lives on your device and stores only static page assets, not your calculation inputs.

Log files, browser data & IP logging

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure and analytics maintain standard log files. These records may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. This information is not linked to anything personally identifiable; it is used for analysing trends, administering the site, and gathering aggregate demographic information.

Analytics

DecideCalc uses Google Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated page-view statistics — browser type, country, page visited, and session duration. No calculator inputs, results, or personally-identifiable information are included in analytics data.

Advertising & Google DoubleClick DART cookie

DecideCalc is enrolled in the Google AdSense program. Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.decidecalc.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in the advertisements and links that appear on DecideCalc, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalise the advertising content that you see on websites you visit. DecideCalc has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers. We never share your calculator inputs with any advertiser — all calculation data stays in your browser. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Cookies

DecideCalc itself sets only functional cookies (theme preference, UI state). Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set their own cookies as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

External links

Some tools offer a "Share on WhatsApp" button. Clicking it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message you can review before sending. We do not transmit your data automatically.

Your privacy rights (GDPR · CCPA · DPDP India)

We ensure compliance with global privacy regulations, granting users rights based on their jurisdiction:

GDPR privacy rights (data protection rights)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), European Union citizens are entitled to: the right to access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to object to processing, and the right to data portability.

CCPA privacy rights (do not sell my personal information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of personal data, and request that a business not sell a consumer's personal data. DecideCalc does not sell personal data.

DPDP India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India, Indian citizens have the right to seek information about personal data processing, request correction and erasure of personal data, and seek grievance redressal regarding data handling practices.

To exercise any of these rights, or for questions about this policy, contact us at our direct support email: contact@decidecalc.com.

Contact form

When you use our contact form, the details you provide (name, email address, topic and message) are transmitted through a third-party form-delivery service (Web3Forms) and delivered to our team's support inbox. They are not stored on our own servers, and they are used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date. Continued use of DecideCalc means you accept the updated policy.
