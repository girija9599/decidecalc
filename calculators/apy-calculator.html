How APY works

Atal Pension Yojana is a Government of India pension scheme. Eligible subscribers join between ages 18 and 40, contribute until 60, and choose a pension of ₹1,000–₹5,000 a month. This calculator uses the published monthly contribution schedule for those choices.

  • Use a savings-bank or post-office account for auto-debit.
  • New APY subscribers who are or have been income-tax payers are not eligible under the current exclusion; check your eligibility before applying.
  • Tax treatment, death/exit rules and enrolment requirements should be checked against current PFRDA or bank guidance.
Important: This tool is educational, not enrolment advice. Confirm current scheme terms with the official PFRDA or your bank.
