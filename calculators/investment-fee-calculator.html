Why a small expense ratio can matter

Fees are charged repeatedly on invested assets, so their impact compounds over time. A fund with a low stated cost is not automatically the right choice: consider benchmark, risk, diversification, tracking difference, taxes and your goal. This calculator isolates the fee effect so you can make a clearer comparison.

Not investment advice: market returns are uncertain and an expense ratio can change. Review the current scheme document and compare like-for-like funds before investing.
