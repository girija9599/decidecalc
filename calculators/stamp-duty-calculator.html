Stamp duty rates snapshot (2025-26)

  • Maharashtra: 6% M / 5% F (urban) + 1% metro cess in Mumbai — often the country's most expensive
  • Karnataka: 5% (>₹45L), 3% (₹20-45L), 2% (<₹20L) — slabbed rates unique
  • Tamil Nadu: 7% + 3% registration (joint highest total)
  • Delhi: 6% M / 4% F + 1% reg — joint (M+F) gets 5%
  • UP: 7% — flat, no discounts
  • Telangana: 7.5% + 1% reg — flat
Rates change frequently. Always verify against the State Revenue Department's latest notification before registering. Use this calculator for estimates, not payment.
