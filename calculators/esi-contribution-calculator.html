For a person ordinarily assessed at the ceiling, coverage is indicated when monthly ESI wages are at or below ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for a qualifying person with disability). If an employee is already covered and earnings cross the ceiling during a contribution period, ESI contributions generally continue through the end of that period; select the continuation control only for that situation.

Payroll caveat: “Wages” under the ESI Act and payroll treatment of allowances can be fact-specific. This estimator does not decide which components your employer must include, and it does not replace the ESIC record, wage register, or professional payroll advice.

Eligibility versus contribution calculation

Eligibility: the selected ₹21,000 or ₹25,000 ceiling helps assess whether an employee falls within ESI coverage on wages. Calculation: once coverage applies, the current rates are calculated on the actual contributory ESI wages entered here, not merely on the ceiling amount. This page therefore shows ₹0 when wages are above the applicable ceiling and no continuation is selected; it does not treat that as a wage cap.

Contribution periods are generally 1 April–30 September and 1 October–31 March. Whether a particular salary change, joining date, wage component, exemption, or establishment is covered should be verified with ESIC or the employer’s payroll team.
