What makes a slug SEO-friendly

  • Hyphens, not underscores — Google treats hyphen as a word separator, underscore as a joiner.
  • Lowercase everything — some servers are case-sensitive; /Guide ≠ /guide (duplicate content risk).
  • Strip stop words — "a", "the", "to" add length without value. Keep numbers — "2026-guide" is searchable.
  • Stay under 60 chars — longer slugs get truncated in search results.
Indian twist: rupee ₹, ®, ™ — non-Latin chars get stripped. "₹10 Lakh in 5 Years" becomes 10-lakh-in-5-years.
