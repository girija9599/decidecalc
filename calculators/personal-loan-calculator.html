How personal loan payments work

  • Unsecured: no collateral required, so rates are higher than auto or home loans.
  • Fixed payment: like a mortgage or auto loan, the payment stays the same every month.
  • Origination fee: some lenders deduct 1–8% upfront; enter that under "fees" to see the real amount you receive.
  • Use wisely: common uses are debt consolidation, medical bills, or home improvement.
Estimate only: Estimate only — your actual rate depends on credit, income, debt-to-income and the lender.
