How to read your Decision Score

0–39 · RED

Don't do it. Major risks or misalignment. Wait, gather more info, or pick a different path.

40–69 · YELLOW

Maybe, with conditions. Address your weakest factors first, then revisit. Time or de-risking may flip this green.

70–100 · GREEN

Go for it. The upside, alignment and reversibility all line up. Take the leap with confidence.

Remember: a score is a thinking aid, not an oracle. Use it to surface what you already half-know. Big irreversible decisions (marriage, kids) deserve extra weight on gut feel & alignment — not just numbers.
