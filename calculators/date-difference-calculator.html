When to use inclusive vs exclusive days?

Exclusive: time between two events (e.g., Jan 1 to Jan 10 = 9 days). Inclusive: count both endpoints (e.g., leave from Jan 1 to Jan 10 = 10 days of leave).

Tip: For interest accrual and legal deadlines, confirm whether the relevant convention is exclusive (most banking) or inclusive (rent, leave).
