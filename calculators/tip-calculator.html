US tipping norms

  • Restaurants: 15–20% is standard; 20%+ for excellent service.
  • Delivery: $3–5 minimum or 15–20%, whichever is higher, especially in bad weather.
  • Rounding: rounding up to a whole number is fine — the difference is tiny.
Estimate only: Tip customs vary; this is only a guideline.
