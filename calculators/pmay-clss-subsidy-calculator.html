How the CLSS subsidy works

Under PMAY-Urban's Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, the interest subsidy is not paid monthly — it is credited to your loan account upfront as a lump sum equal to the Net Present Value (NPV) of all your future EMI savings from the subsidised rate. Your outstanding principal drops immediately, and your EMI falls accordingly.

Who counts as a beneficiary household? A family comprising husband, wife and unmarried children. The household must not own a pucca house anywhere in India, and must not have availed central assistance under any housing scheme before.
Key rule — first pucca house: for EWS/LIG, ownership in the name of a female family member (or co-ownership) is required for new construction. The subsidy applies for purchase, construction, or extension/improvement of the first pucca house.
How is the subsidy credited? Your lender sends the claim to the nodal agency (NHB/HUDCO). The NPV subsidy is released to your loan account upfront — this reduces your principal on day one, which is why the EMI and interest savings compound over the full tenure.
