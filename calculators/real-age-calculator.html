What is "real" (biological) age?

Your chronological age is how many years you've been alive. Your biological age reflects how old your body acts based on lifestyle — sleep, exercise, diet, stress, smoking, alcohol and more. Research shows lifestyle can shift biological age by 10+ years in either direction.

Disclaimer: this is an educational estimate, not a medical diagnosis. For clinical assessment, see a doctor or get an epigenetic/DNA-methylation test.

The 4 habits that age you slowest

  • 7-8 hours of sleep nightly — the single biggest lever.
  • 150+ min/week of moderate exercise — walking counts.
  • Don't smoke, and keep alcohol moderate (≤7 drinks/week).
  • Manage stress — chronic stress measurably shortens telomeres.
