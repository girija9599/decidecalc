How Indian weddings are typically split

Food & venue usually consume the largest chunk, followed by jewelry. Typical percentage allocation:

Category% of Budget
Venue & catering (food)35–40%
Jewelry & gifts18–22%
Decor & event management10–12%
Photography & videography6–8%
Bride & groom attire6–8%
Invitations, transport, music, misc10–12%
Realistic warning: Indian weddings typically overshoot budget by 15-25%. Build a 20% contingency, and agree a hard cap with family before booking starts.
