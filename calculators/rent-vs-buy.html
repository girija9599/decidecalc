How to decide: rent vs buy

This isn't just "EMI vs rent". The real comparison is net worth after the period:

  • Buy path: pay down payment + EMIs + maintenance. Your asset is the appreciated property minus the remaining loan.
  • Rent path: pay rent (rising yearly) + invest the savings (down payment + monthly EMI-rent gap) at your alternative return.
  • If property appreciation beats your alt investment rate, buying usually wins. If not, renting + investing often wins.
Rule of thumb: if annual rent < 3% of property price (rental yield), renting is typically cheaper. Above ~4%, buying looks better.
