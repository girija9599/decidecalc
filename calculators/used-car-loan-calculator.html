How used car financing differs

  • Higher APR: because a used vehicle has less collateral value, lenders typically charge 1–3% more than on new cars.
  • Shorter max terms: many lenders cap used-car loans at 60–72 months, especially on older model years.
  • Lender age limits: some banks will not finance cars over 10 years old or over 100k miles.
  • Tip: a credit-union pre-approval protects you from inflated "buy-here-pay-here" rates.
