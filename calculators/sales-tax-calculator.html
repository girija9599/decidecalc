How sales tax is calculated (US tool)

This is a US-market tool for state and local sales tax. (Shopping for Indian GST instead? Use our GST Calculator — India's 5%/12%/18%/28% slabs work differently.)

Formulas: Add tax: Total = price × (1 + rate ÷ 100). Remove tax: Price = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100) — useful for backing the pre-tax amount out of a receipt.

Worked example: $250 order in Austin, Texas

Austin's combined state + local rate is 8.25%. Adding tax: $250 × 1.0825 = $270.63, of which $20.63 is sales tax. Working backwards from a $270.63 receipt at 8.25%: $270.63 ÷ 1.0825 = $250 — the pre-tax price. Notice the remove-tax formula isn't "subtract 8.25%": that would give $248 — wrong by $2, because tax applies to the pre-tax base, not the total. This distinction matters for expense reports, resale margin math, and filing refunds.

US sales tax: what makes it tricky

Five states charge no sales tax at all (Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, Oregon), while state rates elsewhere run 2.9% (Colorado) to 7.25% (California) — before local add-ons, which push combined rates past 10% in parts of Seattle, Chicago and Louisiana. Three complications matter in practice: locality stacking (state + county + city + special districts each add a slice), category exemptions (groceries are untaxed in many states but taxed in others; clothing is exempt in Minnesota, Massachusetts and New Jersey but not in New York above $110), and destination-based sourcing since the 2018 Wayfair ruling, which obliges online sellers to collect tax in the buyer's state. For a $2,000 laptop, the state-line difference between 0% (Oregon) and 10.25% (parts of Louisiana) is $205 — more than the price of this calculator in interest.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate sales tax on a purchase?

Multiply the pre-tax price by the combined rate and add it: a $60 item at 7% sales tax costs $60 × 1.07 = $64.20. Or use the calculator above with your exact state + local rate. Combined rates include state, county, city and special-district components.

How do I remove sales tax from a total?

Divide the total by (1 + rate): for $214 at 7%, the pre-tax price is $214 ÷ 1.07 = $200, and the tax was $14. Simply subtracting 7% ($198.98) is incorrect — a common expense-report mistake — because the tax was levied on the pre-tax base, not the total.

Which US states have no sales tax?

Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon charge no statewide sales tax. Alaska allows local boroughs to levy their own, so a few Alaskan towns do tax. Note the reverse extremes too: California's 7.25% state rate plus locals makes parts of it the highest in the nation.

Do online purchases carry sales tax?

Usually yes, since the 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair decision: online sellers collect sales tax based on the buyer's delivery address in nearly every state. Marketplaces like Amazon handle it automatically; small out-of-state sellers have thresholds (often $100,000 sales or 200 transactions) before they must collect. You may technically owe use tax on untaxed purchases.

Is sales tax the same as VAT or GST?

No. US sales tax is charged only once, at final sale to the consumer, and the rate varies by locality. VAT/GST systems (like India's GST: 5–28% slabs, or UK VAT at 20%) are charged at every production stage with credits along the way. If you're calculating an Indian invoice, use our GST Calculator instead — the math here doesn't apply.

Are groceries and medicine always taxed?

It varies — one of sales tax's quirks. Most states exempt groceries, but some (Alabama, Mississippi, others) tax them at full or reduced rates; several states tax candy and soda as "junk food" while exempting other food. Prescription medicines are exempt nearly everywhere, while over-the-counter drugs are exempt in some states only. Check your state's revenue department list.
