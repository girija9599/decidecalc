Sales tax basics (US)

  • Rates vary: 0% in OR/MT/NH/DE/AK up to 10.25% in some cities; most states are 4–8%.
  • Add tax: Total = price × (1 + rate%).
  • Remove tax: Price = total ÷ (1 + rate%) — useful to back out the pre-tax amount.
Estimate only: Verify your local rate; online sellers may apply destination-based tax rules.
