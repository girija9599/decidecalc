How counts are calculated

  • Words: sequences separated by whitespace — matching how MS Word counts.
  • Sentences: text split on full stops, question and exclamation marks.
  • Reading time: based on an average 200 words per minute silent-reading speed.
  • Speaking time: based on 130 words per minute — a comfortable presentation pace.
Essay limits: colleges often specify word limits — paste your draft here to check before submission.
