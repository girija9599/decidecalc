How to use this for real goals

  • Exam prep: days remaining ÷ topics = topics/day target
  • Travel: weeks left × save-per-week goal = suitcase fund
  • Debt payoff: total days ÷ ₹you-can-pay-per-day shows if schedule is realistic
  • Habit building: start on a memorable date (birthday, festival) — it's easier to stay consistent
Past dates work too: pick a date in the past (wedding anniversary, first job day) and the counter shows how long it has been — the \"days since\" form is useful for milestone celebrations.
