How EV loan EMI is calculated

EV loans in India — for electric cars, scooters or rickshaws — use the same reducing-balance formula as any vehicle loan, often with a small "green" rate discount:

Formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P = amount financed (on-road price + charger/extras − down payment), r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = months.

Worked example: ₹8,00,000 electric car at 9% for 5 years

A Tata Punch EV or Tiago EV on-road at ₹8,00,000 with a 20% down payment of ₹1,60,000 leaves P = ₹6,40,000. At 9% for 60 months, the EMI is ₹13,285 per month — total repayment ₹7,97,121, of which ₹1,57,121 is interest. With a green-loan discount of 0.25% (8.75%), the EMI drops to ₹13,208 and total interest to ₹1,52,470 — saving ₹4,651 over the tenure. Now the running-cost flip side: charging at home costs roughly ₹1.2–₹1.5 per km versus ₹6–₹8 per km of petrol — on 1,200 km a month, that's ₹7,000–₹8,000 saved monthly, more than the interest cost itself.

EV financing in India: incentives and reality

The subsidy landscape shifted recently: FAME-II closed in March 2024 and was replaced by the PM E-DRIVE scheme (₹10,900 crore, running to March 2026), which subsidises e-two-wheelers (up to ₹5,000), e-three-wheelers, e-buses and e-trucks — but not electric cars. Car buyers instead benefit from state EV policies: Delhi, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and others waive or rebate road tax and registration (worth 4–10% of price) and some offer purchase incentives on cars too. On financing itself, SBI's Green Car Loan, plus HDFC, ICICI, Axis and Kotak EV products, typically discount 0.25–0.5% off the standard car rate — effective rates of 8.5–9.5% in 2026. Lenders fund up to 90–95% of the on-road price including the charger in many cases. Two India-specific cautions: the used-EV resale market is still thin, so an EV's residual value after 5 years is uncertain — keep tenure to 5 years rather than stretching to 7 to avoid financing an asset the market hasn't priced yet; and check whether the home charger plus installation (₹15,000–₹50,000 with wiring) is financed, because it's a genuine part of making the car usable.

Tips before financing an EV

  • Ask for the green discount by name: it's not always advertised — SBI, HDFC and ICICI all have EV variants of their car loans.
  • Check your state's EV policy: road-tax waiver plus registration refund can be worth ₹40,000–₹1,00,000+ on an ₹8 lakh car.
  • Finance the charger if offered: ₹15,000–₹50,000 for a wall box and installation is part of the real cost of ownership.
  • Run the total-cost-of-ownership math: fuel savings of ₹6,000–₹8,000/month at typical commutes usually outweigh the EV's price premium over 5 years.
  • Confirm battery warranty vs tenure: 8-year battery warranties now standard — align loan tenure well inside it so you're never paying EMIs on a degraded pack.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EMI for an ₹8 lakh electric car?

With a ₹1.6 lakh down payment (financing ₹6,40,000) at 9%: about ₹13,285/month over 5 years, or ₹20,352/month over 3 years. With a 0.25% green discount the 5-year EMI drops to ₹13,208. Use the sliders above for your exact model and tenure.

Do banks really offer lower interest rates on EVs?

Yes, typically 0.25–0.5% below the standard car-loan rate — SBI's Green Car Loan, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and Kotak all run EV variants. The saving looks small (₹4,000–₹5,000 over 5 years on a ₹6.4 lakh loan) but it's free money: ask for it explicitly when the rate is being quoted, since it isn't always surfaced by default.

Is there a government subsidy for electric cars in India?

Not at the national level any more — the PM E-DRIVE scheme (2024–2026) covers e-two-wheelers, e-three-wheelers, e-buses and e-trucks, but not electric cars. Car buyers benefit through state EV policies instead: road-tax and registration waivers in Delhi, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Telangana and others, worth 4–10% of the on-road price. Check your state's current transport department notification before purchase.

Can I get an income-tax deduction on EV loan interest?

Section 80EEB allowed a deduction of up to ₹1.5 lakh on EV loan interest, but only for loans sanctioned between 1 April 2019 and 31 March 2023 — it is no longer available for new loans. Current EV financing benefits are the lender's green discount and state-level road-tax waivers, not income-tax relief.

Should I finance an electric scooter or car for a longer tenure?

Keep it at 5 years or less. EV resale values in India are still uncertain (battery-condition-dependent, thin used market), so a 7-year loan risks owing more than the car is worth. Battery warranties of 8 years / 1,60,000 km are now standard — staying inside the warranty window for the whole loan is a sensible guardrail.

Does an EV loan cover the home charger and installation?

Often yes — many banks finance the charger and installation as part of the on-road package (typically ₹15,000–₹50,000 including wiring and a 7.2–7.4 kW wall box), and some manufacturers bundle a charger free with the car. If it's not included, adding roughly ₹25,000 to the loan raises the EMI by only about ₹520 over 5 years — usually worth it versus unsecured borrowing.
