EV financing considerations

  • Green discounts: many banks offer slightly lower APRs for EVs — ask before you accept the standard rate.
  • Tax credits: a federal tax credit can effectively reduce the cost; because the credit arrives after purchase, it does not lower the loan but reduces net spend.
  • Higher price: EVs still cost more upfront; a large down payment keeps the financed amount reasonable.
  • Home charger: remember to budget for installation separately from the loan.
