Compare true cost, not EMI

A lower EMI often hides a longer tenure. Always compare TOTAL outgo = (EMI × months) + processing fee. ₹10K fee and 0.2% rate drop can swing the verdict by lakhs over 20 years.

What to negotiate besides rate

  • Processing fee: negotiable to zero at most private banks (PSU banks hard-coded)
  • Rate reset clause: current EBLR-linked vs old MCLR-linked docs — EBLR passes rate cuts faster
  • Prepayment penalty: floating-rate retail loans have zero penalty by RBI rule — fixed-rate may have 2-4%
  • Insurance bundle: never buy the bundled HLPP — a separate ₹1Cr term plan is cheaper for most
Refinancing math: if a competitor offers 0.5% less, calculate remaining-tenure savings; refinancing fee (typically 0.5-1% + ₹5-15K legal) should be < 1/3rd of savings for the switch to make sense.
