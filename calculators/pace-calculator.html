Predictions use Riegel's formula

T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)^1.06. Trained runners match within 2-3%; beginners typically go 5-10% slower at longer distances because the fatigue factor isn't fully captured. Use the prediction as floor, not goal.

Pace benchmarks for amateur runners

  • Beginner 5K: 7:30 – 8:30 min/km
  • Recreational: 5:30 – 7:00 min/km typical training pace
  • Sub-25 5K: 5:00 min/km
  • Sub-4 marathon: 5:41 min/km sustained
  • Boston qualifier (M <35): 3:00 marathon = 4:16 min/km
Heat penalty: every +5°C above ~10°C slows you ~1-2% per km for races. Most Indian road races run 18-30°C — expect your prediction to look \"fast\" vs what you can actually run on race day.
