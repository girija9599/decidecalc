Why paying only the minimum is expensive

Card minimums normally fall as the balance falls, which lengthens repayment. A fixed amount above the minimum reduces principal earlier, so future interest is charged on less. Avoid adding new purchases while paying down a revolving balance where possible.

Estimate only: issuers calculate interest and minimums differently. Use your statement’s APR, fees, grace-period terms and minimum-payment rule for the most reliable plan.
