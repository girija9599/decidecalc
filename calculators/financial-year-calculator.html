FY vs AY confuses everyone

A salary earned in FY 2025-26 is DECLARED in AY 2026-27. Income tax returns always reference the AY. Business turnover, GST returns and audits reference the FY. Don't mix them up.

Indian fiscal quarter boundaries

  • Q1: Apr 1 – Jun 30
  • Q2: Jul 1 – Sep 30
  • Q3: Oct 1 – Dec 31
  • Q4: Jan 1 – Mar 31
US companies in India: MNCs often follow US calendar quarters (Jan-Mar = Q1, etc.). Check your employer's convention — Q1 for them may be Q4 by Indian tax law.
