How EDD is computed

Naegele's Rule: EDD = LMP + 280 days (40 weeks). Adjusted for longer/shorter cycles: add/subtract the difference from 28 days. If you know your exact conception date, EDD = conception + 266 days.

  • Pregnancy is measured in weeks + days (0w0d → 42w0d).
  • Full term: 39–40 weeks. Late term: 41 weeks. Post-term: >42 weeks (labour usually induced).
  • ±2 weeks variation is normal — only ~5% babies arrived on the exact EDD.
