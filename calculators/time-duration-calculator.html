Common uses

  • Timesheets: net hours after break — enter start/end/lunch for instant payroll decimals.
  • Night shifts: tick "next day" — 22:00 to 06:00 correctly returns 8 hours, not -16.
  • Flight & travel: gate-to-gate time without timezone confusion (same-day flights).
  • Workouts & study: total focused time minus rest intervals.
Decimal shortcut: minutes ÷ 60. 45 min = 0.75 hr, 30 min = 0.5 hr, 15 min = 0.25 hr, 6 min = 0.1 hr.
