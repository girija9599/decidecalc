P-Tax is deductible from income tax

Professional Tax paid is fully deductible from your taxable income under Section 16(iii). So ₹2,500 annual P-Tax actually costs you only ₹1,750 if you're in the 30% bracket (saves ₹750 tax).

P-Tax around India (snapshot 2025)

  • Maharashtra: ₹200/month (>₹25K); November special ₹300 (paid as catch-up)
  • Karnataka: ₹200/month (>₹25K); ₹0 below that
  • West Bengal: ₹0-200/month tiered, capped at ₹2,500/yr statutory max
  • Gujarat: only 4 corporation cities levy it, ₹2,400/yr cap
  • No P-Tax: Delhi, Mumbai-resident non-salaried, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP
Constitutional cap: Article 276 caps Professional Tax at ₹2,500/year per person — no state can exceed this. Most states have plateaued at this ceiling for salaried professionals.
