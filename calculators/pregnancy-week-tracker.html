40 weeks is the average, not the deadline

Only ~5% of babies arrive on their due date. The "term" window is medically 37-42 weeks — anything inside that window is normal. Most first babies come at 41+ weeks.

Trimester guide for Indian mothers

  • 1st trimester (0-13 weeks): folic acid 400mcg daily, dating scan at 7-8 weeks, NT scan at 11-14 weeks
  • 2nd trimester (14-27 weeks): anomaly scan at 18-22 weeks, glucose tolerance at 24-28, GTT test
  • 3rd trimester (28-40 weeks): growth scans every 4-6 weeks, Group B strep at 35-37, hospital bag packed by week 35
Indian context: under Janani Suraksha Yojana, pregnant women in BPL households get ₹1400 (rural) or ₹1000 (urban) as cash assistance for institutional delivery. Registered under PMMVY for additional ₹5000.
