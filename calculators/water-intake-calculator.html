The classic "8 glasses a day" ignores body size & climate. A 50-kg person in a cool climate needs far less than a 90-kg athlete in Chennai summer. Personalise it.

Where the numbers come from

  • Base: ~33 ml per kg of body weight (per European Food Safety Authority guidance)
  • Exercise: +350 ml per 30 min of moderate-intensity exercise
  • Climate: hot/humid adds 300–600 ml via increased sweat
  • Pregnancy/breastfeeding: +700 ml baseline
Fluid ≠ water: tea, coffee, fruit, soups and curries count — roughly 20–30% of typical Indian fluid intake comes from food. Coffee is mildly diuretic but net-positive overall.
