How payoff schedules work

  • Fixed months: we find the payment that zeroes the balance in exactly your chosen number of months.
  • Extra payment effect: adding even $50–100/month cuts months off the timeline and saves significant interest.
  • Avalanche vs snowball: pay highest-rate debts first to minimize interest.
