How the calculation works

This Nepali date difference calculator compares your selected Bikram Sambat (BS) dates and determines the calendar-based difference in years, months and days. Nepali months do not all have the same number of days — a BS month can have 29, 30, 31 or even 32 days depending on the year — so this calculator uses the actual BS calendar data (BS 2000–2090) instead of assuming 30-day months. That is also how your bank counts loan tenure in Nepal.

BS ↔ AD conversion: the same tested conversion engine converts both dates to Gregorian (AD), so the total-day count, the BS↔AD dates and the tenure breakdown always agree with each other.

Use cases

  • Bank loans, home loans, vehicle loans, personal loans and business loans — track elapsed and remaining tenure in BS.
  • EMI/loan tenure tracking — enter the original tenure once and check how much is left as of any date.
  • Contract duration and employment/service duration — joining date to as-of date.
  • Rental agreements and insurance periods.
  • General date calculations — age, anniversaries, project timelines, BS ↔ AD conversion.
Not an official bank record: this tool performs date-duration calculations for planning and reference. It does not replace your bank or lender's official loan records — confirm exact figures with your lender.
