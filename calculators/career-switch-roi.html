How we calculate career-switch ROI

We compare two parallel 5-year paths and find where they cross:

  • Stay path: current salary growing at its rate for 5 years.
  • Switch path: pay training cost + lose income during transition, then earn the higher new salary growing at the new rate.
  • Break-even = the month when cumulative switch income overtakes stay income (accounting for all costs).
  • 5-year net gain = switch cumulative − stay cumulative, discounted to today\'s value.
Smart moves: switch part-time where possible, employer-sponsored upskilling, and choose careers with strong growth rates to compress break-even time.
