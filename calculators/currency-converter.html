Getting the best rate

  • Airport kiosks charge the worst rates — 5–10% worse than interbank. Convert only enough to reach the city.
  • Cards (forex/debit) typically add 1–3.5% over the interbank rate shown here. Check your bank's "markup fee".
  • Wise / Revolut / Niyo-style services usually beat traditional banks for INR conversions.
  • Send on weekdays. Weekends often use Friday's closing rate plus a markup since markets are shut.
