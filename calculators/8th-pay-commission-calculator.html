How the 8th CPC estimate works

  • Fitment Factor: The 7th CPC used 2.57×. Analysts project the 8th CPC factor between 1.82× and 2.86×. This calculator lets you compare all scenarios.
  • Revised Basic Pay: Estimated as (Current Basic + DA) × Fitment Factor. The actual formula may differ.
  • HRA: City X = 20%, City Y = 12%, City Z = 8% of revised basic (current rates; 8th CPC may revise these).
  • Transport Allowance: User-inputted. Current rates vary by pay level and city type.
  • Deductions: NPS, CGHS, CGEGIS, Professional Tax, Income Tax and other deductions are user-configurable.
Not official data: The 8th Pay Commission has not been constituted. No government notification exists. All calculations are projections for planning purposes only. Verify with official sources when the 8th CPC is announced.
