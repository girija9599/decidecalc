How compound interest works

Compound interest earns interest on your interest. The formula is:

Formula: A = P × (1 + r/n)n·t, where P = principal, r = annual rate, n = compounding periods per year, t = years.

Tips

  • Longer > higher rate. 8% for 20 years hugely beats 15% for 5 years.
  • Compounding frequency helps, but slightly. Monthly vs yearly at 8% adds ~0.07% per year.
  • Reinvest interest. Withdrawing interest kills compounding.
