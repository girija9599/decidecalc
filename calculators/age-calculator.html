How is age calculated?

We subtract your birth date from the reference date while accounting for varying month lengths and leap years. Year-month-day breakdown matches the standard civil age used on documents.

Tip: your "completed years" plus months/days is what shows on official forms. Total days/weeks are useful for interest calculations and tenancy agreements.
