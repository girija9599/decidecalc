When to use each style

  • Title Case: blog headlines & chapter names — small words (a, an, the, and, of, in…) stay lowercase.
  • Sentence case: body paragraphs — first letter of each sentence capitalised.
  • camelCase / snake_case: JavaScript / Python variable naming conventions.
  • UPPERCASE: legal disclaimers & form labels (use sparingly — it shouts).
