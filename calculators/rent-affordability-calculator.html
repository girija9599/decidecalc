The 30/10/60 sanity rule

Rent ≤ 30% of take-home · EMIs ≤ 10% · Essentials ≤ 60% combined. Exceed any of those three and you're one surprise expense away from credit-card debt.

Indian rental realities

  • Bengaluru / Mumbai: typical 2BHK ₹25-45K/month — singles often split with flatmates
  • Pune / Hyderabad: 2BHK ₹18-30K — quality of life per rupee is best here in 2026
  • Security deposits: 6-10 months in Bengaluru, 2-3 in most other metros
  • Hidden costs: broker fee ~1 month, move-in furniture ₹30-80K, society transfer ₹5-15K
Rule of thumb: if your rent would exceed 35% after accounting for EMIs and essentials, you can't actually afford it no matter what the listing site says.
