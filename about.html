About DecideCalc

A free, no-login, India-first platform of 120+ working calculators for life's biggest money, career, health & time decisions.

What we believe

Most calculator sites stop at a number. We give you a decision. Every tool ends with a clear result, a comparison, or a green/yellow/red signal — so you actually know what to do next.

Why DecideCalc is different

  • No login, ever. Your data never leaves your device. There is no account storage and no tracking pixels.
  • India-calibrated. GST slabs, tax regimes, city tiers, ₹ lakhs & crores — built for the Indian context.
  • Loads in <2s. Pure HTML, CSS & vanilla JavaScript. Works on any phone, even on slow data.
  • Every tool works. No "coming soon" cards, no broken links. If it's listed, it's functional.

Quantitative methodology & accuracy standards

All calculators deployed on DecideCalc adhere to strict quantitative methodologies. Algorithms are coded using standardized mathematical logic — the same reducing-balance EMI formulas, annuity future-value equations, and statutory tax slabs used across the Indian banking and mutual-fund industry — and executed entirely client-side in JavaScript. To maintain strict accuracy standards, our models are verified against established mathematical benchmarks and worked examples prior to deployment, and pages are re-reviewed whenever statutory rates change. Calculations occur locally within your browser environment to ensure real-time response times and client-side data security.

Privacy & data

Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. Inputs and results are never sent to a server, never logged, and never shared. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
