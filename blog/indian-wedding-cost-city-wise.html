How Much Does an Indian Wedding Really Cost? A City-Wise Budget Breakdown

A 250-guest wedding costs ₹12 lakh in Indore and ₹45 lakh in Mumbai — same rituals, four-times the bill. City-wise benchmarks, category shares, and the levers that actually save money.

Indian weddings are the world’s third-largest consumer-spent events, and the least budgeted. The honest answer to "how much will it cost?" starts with your city and guest count — the same 250-guest function swings from ₹12 lakh to ₹45 lakh on geography alone. Here are the 2026 benchmarks, where the money actually goes, and the five levers that move the total without touching the rituals.

The five levers that actually save

  1. Guest list arithmetic: catering bills per plate (₹1,200–3,500+ in metros). Cutting 50 guests saves ₹1.5–4 lakh per function — the single biggest lever.
  2. Off-peak dates: November–February Saturdays command 20–40% venue premiums. A Thursday in March or a monsoon date can halve venue cost (with a monsoon-weather plan B).
  3. Book 10–12 months out: good venues in every city sell out for the season by mid-year; late bookings pay premium rates or compromise on venue.
  4. Décor re-use across functions: one decorator across mehndi/sangeet/wedding bundles stages and sets at 30–40% below three separate vendors.
  5. Photography packages: the 2-day premium quote often includes a pre-wedding shoot you may not value — unbundle and renegotiate.

Funding it without wrecking your finances

  • Never a personal loan for the full event — at 12–18%, a ₹20 lakh wedding loan costs ₹5–7 lakh in interest over five years: one day of celebration, years of EMI.
  • SIP to a target: a 2-year runway needs about ₹40,000/month for a ₹10 lakh budget at 8% debt-fund returns; use the Savings Goal Calculator for your number.
  • Keep jewellery separate: gold is an asset with resale value — budget it as family investment, not wedding expense.
  • The 1/3 sanity rule: total wedding spend within one-third of annual family income keeps the event from becoming a debt event (see the 50/30/20 framework for the household context).

South Asian destination weddings — the different maths

Udaipur/Goa destination weddings invert the cost structure: venue share falls but per-guest logistics (stay, travel, F&B for 3 days) rise — budgets start around ₹1.5–2 lakh per guest for premium properties. They work best with 75–125 guests; the "smaller, farther, nicer" trade is the entire value proposition.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an Indian wedding cost in 2026?

For a 250-guest, 2-day wedding: ₹8–15 lakh in tier-3 towns, ₹15–30 lakh in tier-2 cities, ₹35–70 lakh in metros like Delhi NCR and Mumbai, and ₹60 lakh+ for destination weddings.

What is the biggest expense in an Indian wedding?

Venue plus catering — 50–60% of the total in most cities. Since venues usually mandate in-house catering, the guest count directly drives this line: every plate costs ₹1,200–3,500+.

Is ₹15 lakh enough for a wedding?

Comfortably in tier-2/3 cities for 200–300 guests with mid-market choices. In metros, ₹15 lakh means a smaller guest list or single-venue format.

Should I take a loan for a wedding?

Avoid it if possible — a ₹20 lakh personal loan at 12–18% adds ₹5–7 lakh in interest. Prefer a 12–24 month savings plan (SIP/short-term FD ladder) sized with the Savings Goal Calculator.

How do destination wedding costs compare?

Venue share drops but logistics rise — premium destination weddings run ₹1.5–2 lakh per guest for stay, travel and F&B over 3 days. They suit 75–125 guests best.
